2.  **The Story of Achan Chah and Samadhi at the Kathen Ceremony**
    *   **Story:** During a Thai festival (Kathen) where young women are beautifully dressed, Achan Chah asks his student, Samadhi, what he thinks. Samadhi replies, "I like it, but I don't want it."
    *   **Meaning:** This is cited as the "best expression of Paticcasamuppada." It demonstrates a wise response to a pleasant feeling (liking). The unwise reaction would be either to deny the liking (objectification/celibacy) or to act on the desire (wanting it). The wise middle path is to acknowledge the feeling of liking with clarity, but to not let it escalate into unwholesome craving or grasping. It’s a moment of freedom and clear seeing.
