### Summary

This Dhamma talk is a warm, supportive conversation between a student (Steve, **Speaker B**) and a teacher (Dhammarato, **Speaker A**), who is reconnecting after about three years. The core themes revolve around overcoming hindrances, restarting meditation practice, and finding security and joy in the present moment.

Here is a summary of the key points:

**1. Reconnection and Addressing Hindrances:** Steve initiates the call, expressing that he felt ashamed or insecure, which kept him from reaching out. Dhammarato immediately reframes this, welcoming him back and emphasizing that the Dhamma's goal is **security and joy**, not shame. He compares feeling ashamed to being in debt—a state of insecurity—and declares that in their Dhamma relationship, no one owes anything.

**2. The Central Teaching: Security and the End of Suffering:** Dhammarato reiterates the Buddha's core teaching: the understanding of *dukkha* (suffering/stress) and its end. He links this directly to Steve's life experience, noting that even buying a house (a form of worldly security) doesn't bring 100% peace. True security comes from the mind. Anything that prevents joy is *dukkha*, and the path is about seeing that *dukkha* clearly to be free from it.

**3. Remembering Past Success & Restarting Practice:** Steve recalls that during their previous talks and practice, he experienced clarity and happiness, anchored in the present moment.
