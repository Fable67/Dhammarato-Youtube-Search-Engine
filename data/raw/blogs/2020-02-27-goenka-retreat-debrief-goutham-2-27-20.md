- Goenka retreats are mostly indoor sitting; Dhammarato advocates for walking meditation as a way to stay rooted in the body.

**6. The Student’s Realization:**

**8. Final Advice:**
- Dhammarato encourages the student to **practice being successful and secure** in each moment, to change attitude rather than circumstances, and to continue sharing joy, as it reinforces one's own practice.

**Overall Theme:** The talk bridges the Goenka method with deeper Anapanasati teachings, emphasizing that happiness and satisfaction are **skills to be cultivated intentionally**, not passive results of technique. The core teaching: *You can be happy with this breath, right now.*

    - **Purpose:** To argue that the core of any religion (including Christianity) is seclusion and inner work, and that modern Christianity has lost this essence, becoming like the Pharisees.
