3.  **Eradication of Doubt (Third Fetter):** The most transformative doubt to overcome is thinking "someone else will fix me." The noble, supramundane realization is: **"No matter how obstructed the mind gets, I can get myself out of this mess."** This is the first step on the Noble Path.

- **Key Teaching:** Do not practice *wanting* to be satisfied; **practice *being* satisfied** in the present moment. This is the direct way to cultivate the factors of *Jhana* (first absorption) and *Anapanasati*.
- This is like learning the piano: you don't carry a violin; you actually play the piano. Practice the feeling you want to achieve.
- The goal of Buddhism is not escape, but coming to a state of **complete contentment, satisfaction, and lightness.**
