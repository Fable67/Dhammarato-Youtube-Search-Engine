**11. The Raft and the Flood (Speaker A)**
- **Context:** Describing the vastness of the path and the initial overwhelming nature of hindrances.
- **Metaphor (from Sutta 22):** The Dhamma is like a **raft** built to navigate a **roaring flood**. The hindrances are the flood.
- **Meaning:** The initial practice feels like being "flooded" with hindrances. The practice itself (the raft) allows you to navigate this chaotic state and reach the "other shore" (Nibbana). The "first knowledge" is the "can-do attitude" that you can handle the flood.
