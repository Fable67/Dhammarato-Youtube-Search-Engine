**Key Instructions & Concepts:**

The goal is to move from a "survival mode" (shallow, frozen breath) to being **"vibrantly alive."**

    - **Deep, Full Breath (Step 1):** A long, deliberate in-breath filling the lungs completely (chest, diaphragm, shoulders), followed by a long, sighing out-breath. This is the primary tool for daily practice to build energy and cleanse the body (like pranayama/yoga breathing).
